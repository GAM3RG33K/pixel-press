# pixel-press — brand identity

## Concept
**"The Press"** — the product compacts video on-device, like a printing press applies
measured, even pressure. Two fused ideas:

- **Press** — the core metaphor the brand is named for (pixel-press)
- **Pixel** — the atom of video; three scan bars compressing into a violet plate

The mark is three white scan bars of decreasing width (264 / 184 / 104) stacked above a
single wider violet "press plate" — the whole thing centered in a near-black rounded-square badge.
No arrows, no lightning, no clapperboards — compression, not download.

## Files
- `pixel-press-logo.svg` — primary mark (same as `public/icon.svg`)
- `public/icon.svg` — app icon source of truth
- App icons are regenerated with `bun run icons` (rsvg-convert + iconutil + Pillow)

## Design tokens
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--badge` | `#0D0D12` | badge/plate background, near-black |
| `--bar`   | `#FFFFFF` | scan bars |
| `--plate` | `#8B5CF6` | press plate accent (matches app `primary`) |
| `--fg`    | `#FFFFFF` | wordmark on dark |
| `--muted` | `#A6A6B0` | captions on dark |

Badge corner radius: 22% (rx=113 on a 512 grid). App window chrome and tauri icons derive
from the same SVG. Monospace accents in UI stay `ui-monospace`.

## Wordmark
"pixel-press" — all lowercase, tight tracking (`-0.02em`), semibold weight, white on dark.
Usage: `pixel-press` next to the mark; never "PixelPress", never "Pixel Press", never with an emoji.

## Usage rules
- Place the mark on `--badge` or transparent; give it clear space ≥ 25% of badge width.
- Never recolor the bars/plate; never stretch; never add shadows or glows.
- Minimum size: 32×32 px (bars stay legible).
- Sound-alikes to avoid: downward download arrows, play buttons, film clappers, lightning bolts.

## Tagline
"Compress like you mean it."
