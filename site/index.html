Compress like you mean it.

pixel-press is a local-only desktop app that shrinks your videos with native FFmpeg and an optional on-device LLM. Nothing uploads. No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud. The four upstream workflows — compress, compare, audit, scan & optimize — as one quiet workbench.

01 — The Workbench

Four tools.
Zero cloud.

Every byte processed on your machine. Rule engine always works — the LLM is opt-in, offline, and ~210 MB once.

S.01

Smart Compress

Drop any video. Native ffprobe reads it, the rule engine (or the on-device LLM) picks codec and CRF, native ffmpeg encodes. Natural-language intent bar included; codec fallbacks are loud, never silent.

C.02

Visual & Metric Compare

Split-screen slider over synchronized samples. Size, SSIM, bitrate deltas across anchor variants — pick the one that wins.

A.03

Project Video Auditor

Point at your project folder. Flags missing muted / playsinline / preload / poster, oversized dimensions — tag and line for everything.

O.04

Scan & Optimize

Batch-encode every flagged video in place, generate updated HTML/JSX snippets, export a zip. One pass, whole project.

L.05

Local AI — your machine only

Optional SmolLM2-360M (Apache-2.0, ~210 MB GGUF) via llama.cpp. Plans encodes and explains choices. If it’s not installed, the rule engine carries the whole app. Nothing ever leaves the device.
